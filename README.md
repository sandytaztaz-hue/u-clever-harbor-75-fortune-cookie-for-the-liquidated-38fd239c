## The entry

One fortune cookie line for a crypto trader who has just been liquidated —
17 words, 94 characters, plain text, read whole in a single breath:

> Your portfolio is gone, but this cookie is now your most valuable holding. Do not leverage it.

Why this line: the cookie speaks in its own small oracular voice about the
loss, then breaks it with the last phrase — "leverage" is the trader's word
turned back on the one thing he has left, rueful rather than cruel. The field
is full of lucky numbers that are all zero, great fortunes sitting in someone
else's wallet, and tall dark strangers who work for the exchange; nobody tells
the trader the cookie itself is the portfolio now — and warns him not to
leverage it.

## How it runs

`main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`. Deterministic: the same
single line on every run, no randomness, no network, no clock.
